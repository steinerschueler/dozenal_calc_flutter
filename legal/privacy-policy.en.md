# Privacy Policy — Dozenal Calc

**Last updated:** 2 May 2026

## Who is behind the app?

Dozenal Calc is developed and published by **Eric Naville**, Bern, Switzerland. For questions about this policy or the app, you can reach me at: dozenal@weltanschauung.app.

## What data is collected?

**None.** The app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data whatsoever. It runs entirely locally on your device and does not communicate with any servers.

## What permissions does the app request?

Dozenal Calc requests no Android permissions — no access to camera, microphone, location, contacts, storage, or the internet. The app runs offline and always will.

## Is data shared with third parties?

No. Since nothing is collected, there is nothing to share.

## Tracking, analytics, advertising

The app contains **no advertising**, **no tracking SDKs**, and **no analytics packages**. Your behaviour is not observed in any way.

## Age suitability

The app is suitable for all age groups.

## Open-source components used

The app is built with the Flutter framework (Google) and uses, among others, the open-source packages `flutter_markdown` and `cupertino_icons`. All included components likewise operate locally and transmit no data.

## Changes to this policy

Should future versions of the app introduce features that require data collection, this policy will be updated in advance, and the app will clearly indicate that a change has taken place. The date at the top of this page always shows the current version.

## Contact

For questions about this privacy policy: dozenal@weltanschauung.app.
